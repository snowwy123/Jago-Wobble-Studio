# Make a little brush pack

A stamp is a small image that your brush repeats. A preset saves that image and its settings. A brush pack lets you share a collection of those presets.

## Make a stamp

Draw a mark and select it with any selection tool. In **Draw > Stamp brushes > Edit brush**, choose **Create stamp from selection**. Or use **Import stamp image** to bring in a PNG. A transparent background keeps the edges clean.

Choose the stamp under **Brush > In this drawing**. The app captures a still image, so any motion in your selection is not part of the stamp.

## Give it some character

Try size, opacity, colour, spacing, rotation, scatter and size variation. Stamp size goes up to 160 px. **Keep original image colours** uses the image's colours; turn it off to use your drawing colour.

Use **One stamp per click** for single marks or **Along a stroke** for a trail. Built-in pattern brushes also offer continuous patterns, pattern size and pattern angle. Imported PNG stamps use brush size to scale the whole image.

Choose **Save as new preset** below the stamp controls, then give it a name. You can also save from **Stamp presets**. This saves the stamp setup, even if you last used a normal pen. Normal pen, pencil, marker and spray settings are not saved as stamp presets.

## Share it

1. Make and save the stamps you want in your pack.
2. Open **Stamp presets** and choose **Export library**. The .jagopresets file includes your saved stamp and motion presets, with their custom images.
3. Other people choose **Import library**. The presets appear in the Brush and Motion preset menus, ready to use.

Use marks you made or have permission to redistribute, and include any required credits or licences.

## Keep things tidy

Imported presets and your own saved presets have separate groups in the menus. Editing one shows **(modified)**. Save it under a new name to keep your version.

In the library, select several entries and use **Delete selected**, or remove one with its × button. **Undo removal** brings back the last removed group while the app is open. Removing a preset does not remove marks from your drawings.

A motion preset is a saved setup for an existing wiggle type. For example, **Paper Breeze · Flutter** is a particular Flutter setup. Use **Apply to strokes** or **Apply to layer** to change existing artwork.

## A few practical bits

- PNG imports must be under 4 MB and at most 4096 pixels per side. Stamp images are reduced to fit within 128 × 128 pixels, so clear, simple marks work best.
- A project holds up to 24 custom stamp images. Your library holds up to 30 stamp presets and 30 motion presets.
- Projects include the stamp images they use. Your saved preset library is separate, so export it as a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.
