# Meet the odd little marks

These are starting points. A small change in spacing, opacity or size can turn the same stamp into something quite different.

| Brush | Have a go at... | A useful tweak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crumb Collector | Rough edges, dusty shadows and scruffy ground. | Lower opacity and build up a few passes for a softer texture. |
| Dry Little Drag | Dry ink ribbons, grass or scratches across a surface. | Turn Rotate along the stroke off for marks that all face the same way. |
| Puddle Jumper | Patchy pools of ink, loose foliage and little blobs. | Reduce spacing to join the puddles into a broader brush stroke. |
| Coffee Break | Broken rings, loose wheels or messy page decorations. | Choose One stamp per click and try a larger size. |
| Threadbare | Stray fibres, fuzzy edges and light cross-shading. | Paint a second pass at another rotation for a tangled texture. |
| Sidewalk Scribble | Scruffy little bushes, curls and restless pencil shading. | Reduce spacing to overlap the scribbles into a denser patch. |
| Loose Ends | Wispy fur, grassy tufts and trailing threads. | Use short strokes that curve away from the edge of a shape. |
| Paper Freckles | Gentle dusty shading and soft patches of grain. | Build slowly with repeated passes; raise opacity for stronger dust. |
| Wonky Confetti | Little celebrations, patterned clothes and scattered scraps. | Lower scatter to keep the scraps closer to your line. |
| Little Sparks | Bright ideas, shiny things and a bit of magic. | Choose One stamp per click for deliberate accents. |
| Bubble Trouble | Loose bubbles, bubbly borders and underwater doodles. | Increase spacing for separate clusters, or lower it for a bubbly trail. |
| Wobbly Teeth | Jagged trims, little scales and silly monster edges. | Draw slowly around a curve with Rotate along the stroke on. |
| Pocket Fern | Little plants, leafy borders and sprigs around lettering. | Choose One stamp per click for separate plants, or increase scatter for a leafy patch. |
| Loose Stitches | Crooked seams, soft toy details and sewn borders. | Keep Rotate along the stroke on and draw a curve. Adjust spacing if you want gaps between stitch groups. |
| Thumb Thing | Swirly print textures, odd spots and little contour marks. | Try One stamp per click at a large size, then turn each print with Stamp rotation. |
| Tape It Together | Torn paper strips, patched corners and scrapbook details. | Choose One stamp per click and lower opacity for overlapping strips. |

## Four little experiments

**A scruffy moon:** draw a plain circle, use Paper Freckles to shade one side, add a few Coffee Break rings as craters, and place Little Sparks around it. Put the shading on its own layer so you can lower its opacity.

**A windy little patch:** draw short strokes with Loose Ends, add Crumb Collector underneath, then try Paper Breeze on the grass layer. Keep the amount small for a gentle flutter.

**A very silly creature:** draw a simple blob with your usual pen, add Wobbly Teeth around one edge and use Bubble Trouble for odd little spots. A few marks are often enough.

Turn off **Wiggle new marks** when you want to judge the brush texture without movement. Add the motion after you like the drawing.

**A page from your pockets:** place a few Pocket Fern sprigs, add a strip of Tape It Together at a corner, then draw a Loose Stitches border. Thumb Thing makes an odd little printed accent beside a handwritten note.

To keep your experiment, choose **Save as new preset** at the bottom of the stamp controls. Your new setup is saved separately from the imported original.
