Make your own brush pack

A stamp is a small shape or image you can paint with. For example, turn a drawn star into a stamp, then click to place one star or drag to paint a trail of stars.

  1. Create a stamp. Draw a small mark, then use Rectangle selection or Lasso to select it on its layer. Open Draw > Stamp brushes > Edit brush and choose Create stamp from selection. Or choose Import stamp image to use a PNG. A transparent background avoids a box around the stamp.
  2. Try it out. Choose your stamp from Brush. Adjust size, spacing and colour, then click Use stamp brush (J) and draw. Spacing controls the gaps between repeated marks.
  3. Save the setup. Open Stamp presets, enter a name and choose Save stamp preset. A preset remembers the stamp image and settings shown in the dialog. It saves that stamp setup even if you last used a normal pen.
  4. Make a pack. Repeat for any other stamps, then choose Export library in Stamp presets. This downloads all your saved stamp and motion presets as one .jagopresets file, including the custom stamp images they use.
  5. Share or reload it. Use Import library in Stamp presets once. All brushes are then available under Brush in Draw, and motions under Motion preset in Motion. Choose a name to use its settings.

Creating a stamp captures a still image, without its animation. Save movement separately with Save motion preset. Custom stamp images fit inside 128 × 128 pixels. A project holds 24 custom images; a library holds 30 stamp and 30 motion presets. Export a backup to keep or share. Use marks you own or have permission to share.

Save as new preset

Keep this stamp image, colour, size and other brush settings as your own preset. The original stays unchanged.

Stamp & motion presets

Import a pack once. All its brushes appear in Brush in Draw, and its motions appear in Motion preset in Motion. Choose one to use it. Images are included automatically when needed; there is no separate add-to-project step.

Save stamp preset keeps the stamp image and settings shown below, even when a normal pen is selected. Save motion preset keeps the current movement setup. Export library backs up all your saved presets.

Tick presets to remove them together. Removing presets does not remove images or marks from saved drawings. Earlier presets have no recorded origin. Reimporting their pack moves matching entries into Imported without adding duplicates.

On drawing canvases, switch between Smooth and Pixel above the canvas. Pixel size changes the block size without changing your strokes. For pixel art, choose a Pixel art canvas in New: draw directly with a 1 px pen, zoom up to 4000%, and export crisp pixels. Pixel art canvases keep a fixed 1 px grid. The shapes button opens the extra shapes; Filled shape applies to new shapes.

The Move tool moves selected pixels when there is a selection, or the whole active layer when there is none. Paste returns artwork to its original position. Hold Shift to move in one direction. Fill can use the active layer or all visible artwork as a boundary. Erasing affects only the active layer. Use the layer lock to protect your work. Layer motion also moves imported images.

Stamp brush: J. Rectangle selection: U. Lasso: K. Copy/cut/paste selection: Ctrl C / X / V. Motion, shading and recovery live in the inspector tabs.

Motion study: the pendulum sways from its top pivot, the hatched ribbon ripples and a loose pencil line flutters. A soft shadow breathes twice per swing. Select Pendulum in Layers, open Motion, choose Edit active layer settings, then drag the pivot cross or use Place pivot on canvas. Preview it, then Apply to layer to keep the change.

Shading study: toggle the five named layers to explore the cast shadow, pencil base, stipple, hatching and highlight. Clipped shading follows the gently moving ball. Try Smooth and Pixel.

Take it to the print table

Wobble → Dither: export a PNG sequence ZIP, then in Jago Dither Studio choose Layers → + Wobble loop. Your moving drawing becomes an image layer, ready for printing textures, colour and lettering.

Dither → Wobble: export a Wobble project from Dither and open the .jago file here. Choose one printed layer or separate ink layers. Add a drawing layer above it to keep sketching. Keep your original .jagodither file to edit the text and print settings.

Dither accepts loops up to 12 seconds. The return trip supports up to 48 frames; choose a lower transfer rate for longer loops. Click the paper dimensions above the drawing to resize it without stretching your marks.
